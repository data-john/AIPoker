Subject: Licensing question: offline desktop poker game with local AI opponents

Hello,

I am building an offline Linux desktop poker game (six-max No-Limit Hold'em, virtual chips only,
no real-money play, no online component) with AI opponents of varying strength. I have been
reading the NoRegrets repository and have a few licensing questions before I commit to anything.

1. Commercial edition: what are the current price and term, and does it include the right to

2. Self-trained blueprints: if I train my own blueprint using the MIT-licensed public source, may I

3. Use restrictions: the published terms mention restrictions around non-consenting human
   opponents and real-money platforms. Can you confirm that these do not apply to a single-player,
   offline game where the human knows they are playing against AI opponents?
4. Scope: are there limits on the number of installations, territories, updates, or support
   obligations I should plan for?

I would appreciate your answers in writing. Thank you for publishing the project.
